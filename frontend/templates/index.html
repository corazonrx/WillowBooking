A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Before you
make yourself at home.

A smoother arrival starts here.

When can I check in and out?+

Choose an arrival window between 14:00 and 22:00. Your room is yours until 11:00 on your departure day. All times are local to Warsaw.

How is my room selected?+

Choose Classic, Deluxe or Family. We will reserve an available room in your preferred category and include the room number in your confirmation.

Can I change my plans?+

You can cancel from Your stay before your selected arrival window begins. To change your dates, cancel your existing reservation and make a new one, subject to availability.

How do reservations work?+

Create an account, choose your dates and confirm your room. View and manage your reservations from Your stay.
